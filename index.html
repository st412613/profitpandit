Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — the profit calculator and zero-fee checker are free, forever. Advanced features (sales estimates, keyword research, competitor tracking) will come later as an optional Pro plan.

Are the numbers exact?

They are close estimates based on Amazon.in's published fee structures (updated for the March 2026 changes). Your exact fees depend on your sub-category and account — always verify on Seller Central before big decisions.

Do you see my Amazon account or data?

No. The extension has no access to your Amazon account. It only reads the public price and category of the page you're viewing, and all calculations happen inside your browser.

Which marketplaces are supported?

Amazon.in today. Meesho and Flipkart support are on the roadmap.
